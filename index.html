    How to fish

    1. Cast. Hold to load the rod, then let go when the power bar is high. Longer casts reach deeper water and bigger fish.
    2. Hook. Little twitches are just nibbles. When the bobber goes all the way under, tap right away.
    3. Reel. Hold to reel. When the fish makes a run, let go or the line snaps. Leave it slack too long and it shakes the hook.

    Time of day and season both change what bites. Holidays bring their own one of a kind catches, and exotic fish are the rarest of all. Sell what you catch for coins and spend them in the tackle shop.
