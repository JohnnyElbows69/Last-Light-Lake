# Last Light Lake

A relaxing fishing game that runs right in your browser. Fish your way through a full year on a mountain lake: the seasons change, holidays bring surprises, and the rarest catches are worth chasing.

**Play it here:** https://JohnnyElbows69.github.io/Last-Light-Lake/

## How to play

1. **Cast.** Press and hold, then let go when the power bar is high. Longer casts reach deeper water and bigger fish.
2. **Hook.** Little twitches are just nibbles. When the bobber goes all the way under, tap right away.
3. **Reel.** Hold to reel. When the fish makes a run, let go or the line snaps.

Works with a mouse, touch, or the Space bar. Press P to pause.

## Features

- 12 species of fish, from common perch to legendary sturgeon and 4 rare exotics
- A full day and night cycle with a 365 day calendar and four seasons
- Holiday events with their own decorations and one of a kind catches
- A tackle shop with rods and bobbers that change how you fish
- Rare catches get flashier effects the rarer they are
- Night sounds, birds, splashes, and reel clicks, all generated in the browser
- Progress saves automatically in your browser

## Running it yourself

The whole game is one file, `index.html`. Download it and open it in any modern browser, no install needed.
